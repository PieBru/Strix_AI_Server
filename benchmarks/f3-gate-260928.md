old build's 12/12. The 95 % CI of the 11/12 samples tops out at 0.985, i.e. it
does not include a perfect 12/12 — but the baseline 12/12 is itself a single

instruction-following battery; the primary coding battery (fcb15, 15 items,

matters: one A/A iten12 run on the baseline build to measure its own variance,
